## Notable: how this snapshot differs from the previous run

In the immediately previous commit (`255e4e0`), `classify_delivery`
returned `"unknown"` for listings whose chip text was silent (e.g.
`Тариф «Макс» | 1 месяц`) and whose title was just
`Z.AI | Lite • Pro • Max | Подписка на 1 месяц`. Those listings were
hidden from the cheapest table even though their prices (12 890 ₽,
15 600 ₽, 15 811 ₽) were the cheapest available.

`558169a` adds the missing title patterns (`Мгновенная активация`,
`оперативная поддержка`, `Гарантия 100%`, `Поддержка 24/7`,
`моментальная выдача`) and a title-based LLM fallback that infers
`new_account` from neutral titles and `own_account` from titles like
`ПОДПИСКИ Z.AI / НА ВАШ АККАУНТ`. The numbers above are the result.
